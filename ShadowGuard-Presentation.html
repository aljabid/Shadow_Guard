The Problem
01

Regulators are reactive, never predictive

The AFM receives complaints only after victims lose money. By the time an investigation begins, crypto exchanges have vanished, wallets are emptied, and operators have fled.

02

Digital crime spans fragmented, unmonitored channels

Dropper recruitment on Telegram, drug sales on DarkNet, pyramid promotions on Instagram, crypto laundering on TRON — no unified monitoring layer exists across these platforms.

03

No cross-channel intelligence correlation

The same wallets, Telegram handles, and domains appear across multiple crime types but are never cross-referenced in real time. Evidence fragments stay siloed.
